Installation guide

Signed APK with phone and tablet layouts. No desktop lyric overlays, plugins or Local API in this version.

Check the SHA-256 files in the Release before installing. Checksums detect corruption; they do not independently verify the publisher. Desktop auto-update feeds are currently x64 only.

Make room for your ideas

A player with
your own touches.

Extend the desktop app with styles, lyric scenes or platform components. Download a .yaqmc-plugin file, then install and review its permissions in Settings → Plugins.

Examples are disabled by default and are unverified local sideloads, not a marketplace. Platform examples use synthetic data, not a real music service.
